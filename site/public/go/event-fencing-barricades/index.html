Questions

Event fencing questions.

Who rents event fencing from you?

Festival organizers, race directors, concert promoters, farmers markets, county fairs, corporate event planners, breweries running outdoor events, and venues that host events on their own grounds.

Can you set fencing overnight or early in the morning?

Yes. Event schedules are the reason this business is built the way it is. Tell us the load-in window and we work to it, including overnight and before dawn.

Can I rent barricades without any fence?

Yes, and plenty of events do. Barricades are their own line. Tell us the linear footage of the queue line or the stage front and we can price it quickly.

Can you print our sponsors on the windscreen?

Yes. Custom printed windscreen fits across a panel run and turns the perimeter into a banner the length of the site. Windscreen is sold rather than rented, so it's yours afterwards.

What do you need to quote an event?

The run of show times for load-in, doors and load-out, the footprint, and roughly how much line you need. Where the crowd flows and where vehicles cross the line matter more than the exact footage.
