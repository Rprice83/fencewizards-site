Questions

Rental questions.

How much notice do you need?

24 to 48 hours is the normal window and it covers most jobs. More notice always makes the install cleaner, because we can plan the run rather than improvise it.

Is taking the fence back down included?

Yes. The removal is already inside the number you agreed at the start, and we pull it on your schedule rather than ours. There is no collection charge at the end.

Will panels work on asphalt or concrete?

Yes. Panels sit in stands weighted with sandbags, so they don't need ground that takes a driven post. That is usually the deciding factor on a paved lot or a downtown site.

Do you do residential fencing?

No. Ninety-nine percent of what we do is business to business, and we would rather say that on the call than after you have waited for a quote.
