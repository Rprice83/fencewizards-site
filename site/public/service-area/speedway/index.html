Speedway is a crowd-control town, and May is the reason.

The Indianapolis Motor Speedway and everything that happens around it turn a small town into one of the largest gatherings in the country, and the fencing that goes with it is barricades and panel runs rather than construction perimeters: queue lines, vendor rows, hospitality footprints, parking separation and keeping vehicles away from people on foot.

Local know-how

What's different about Speedway.

The Main Street district works the same way on a smaller scale through the rest of the year. Street events, hospitality build-outs and temporary footprints on a public street all need a line that holds a crowd and comes out clean afterwards, set to the times on the plan rather than to a normal working day.

Printed windscreen is worth mentioning here more than anywhere else in the radius. A perimeter around a hospitality area or a vendor row is a surface people are going to look at for a whole weekend, and printing it with a sponsor or a brand costs a fraction of what the same square footage of signage would cost any other way.

Questions

Questions we get about Speedway.

Three that come up on nearly every call from this part of the radius.

Can you handle event work during race month?

This is exactly the kind of work barricades and panel runs exist for. Give us the footprint, where the crowd flows, where vehicles cross the line and the times for set and strike, and we plan the crew and the truck around your schedule rather than around office hours.

Do you set barricades on a public street?

Yes. Interlocking steel barricades hold a queue or a crowd edge without touching the surface, indoors or out, and they come out clean when the event is over. Tell us the linear footage of the line and what it is holding back and we can price it quickly.

Can we put sponsor graphics on the fence?

Yes, and this is the town to do it in. Custom printed windscreen fits across a panel run and turns the perimeter into a banner the length of the footprint. It is sold rather than rented, so it is yours afterwards and it goes back up next year.
