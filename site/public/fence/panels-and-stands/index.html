Fence types

Temporary fence panels and stands.

Six-foot panels in sandbagged stands, on site in 24 to 48 hours, and light enough for two of your crew to shift a section by hand.

Temporary fence panels sit in sandbagged stands on top of the ground.

That means a run can be installed in a fraction of the time a driven fence takes, and moved by two people when the site plan changes. It's our bread and butter, and it's the right answer for most construction sites, most events and almost every emergency call.

Where it fits

Where panels are the right call.

Construction sites that keep changing

Groundwork moves faster than a site plan does. Panels let a superintendent open a new access point in the morning and close it again in the afternoon.

Events on a tight window

A panel run goes in during a load-in window and comes out during load-out, which is the schedule an event fence has to keep.

First response after a loss

On day one nobody has a measured plan. Panels get a perimeter closed now and can be adjusted once the site has been assessed properly.

Questions

Questions on panels and stands.

What buyers ask before they order this one, including the jobs it's the wrong answer to.

Do you offer eight-foot panels?

Eight-foot panels and stands are a special order, as is eight-foot post-driven chain link. Six-foot is the standard. If the job needs the extra height, tell us early and we will confirm availability when we quote.

Can my own crew move a section?

Yes, and most of them do. That portability is the reason to choose panels over a driven fence. If a whole run needs relocating, call us and we will come out, same day for general contractors.

Will panels stand up on asphalt or concrete?

Yes, and this is usually the deciding factor. The panels sit in stands weighted with sandbags, so a paved lot, a plaza or a downtown sidewalk is no obstacle at all.

How much wind will a panel run take?

Sandbagged stands are what hold the line, so it depends on how many bags are on it and whether windscreen is fitted. Windscreen turns a fence into a sail, which is why we ask about it when we price the run.

Can windscreen be added to a panel run later?

Yes. Windscreen fits across an existing run for privacy and dust control, plain or custom printed. It's sold, not rented, so it stays yours when the fence goes back.

Is this the right choice for a site that has to stay shut overnight?

Often it isn't. Panels are portable by design, which means someone determined can move them too. Where the perimeter has to resist being walked through, post-driven chain link is the better choice, and we will say so on the call.
