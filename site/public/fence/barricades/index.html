Barricades are a different job from fencing.

They shape where people stand, queue and cross, and they have to be set where the production plan says rather than dropped in a pile for somebody else to sort out. We deliver interlocking steel barricades across the Indianapolis metro for events, corporate functions, bars, nightclubs and venues.

Where it fits

Where barricades are the right call.

Queue and entry lines

The line into a venue is where crowd problems start. Barricade it properly and the door team can actually work.

Stage and bar fronts

Interlocking steel takes a crowd leaning on it. Plastic and stanchions don't, which is why we don't carry them.

Vehicle and pedestrian separation

Barricades combined with a panel run keep vehicles and people apart on a site where both are moving at once.

Questions

Questions on barricade rental.

What buyers ask before they order, including the jobs barricades are the wrong answer to.

What are these actually for?

Queue lines, stage fronts, bar and beverage areas, and keeping vehicles away from people. They interlock into a line that holds a crowd rather than just marking where the crowd should be.

Can I rent barricades without any fence?

Yes, and plenty of events do. Barricades are their own line and a smaller ticket than a fence run. Tell us the linear footage of the queue or the stage front and we can price it quickly.

Do you set them where the plan says, or just drop them off?

We set them to your plan. Dropping a stack of steel in a parking lot for somebody else's crew to sort out isn't a delivery, and on event day it's the difference between a line that works at doors and one that doesn't.

Can you work to a production schedule?

Yes, including overnight and before dawn. Load-in, doors and load-out are the three times the plan follows, rather than a generic install window.

Do you supply them indoors as well as outside?

Yes. Corporate functions, bars, nightclubs and venue interiors are normal work for these, and they go in and out without touching the floor structure.

How quickly can you turn an order around?

24 to 48 hours is the normal window, the same as fence. Event work often moves faster than that, so call and tell us the date rather than assuming it's too late.
