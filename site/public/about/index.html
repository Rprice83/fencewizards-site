Fence Wizards is a family business.

Richard Warren owns it, his father and his brother work in it every day, and he is a third-generation fencer. The ten-plus years of permanent fence work behind this company really go back a lot further than that.

Equipment & buying

Why the equipment matters.

We buy the most current fence installation equipment we can, and the reason isn't that it's impressive. It's that the crew gets more done in a day and finishes it less beaten up. Injury and fatigue are what turn a two-day install into a four-day one.

We also do our own procurement, straight from the manufacturer rather than through a distributor. That takes a layer of cost out, and the saving goes into the quote rather than into the margin.

Richard is working on his OSHA 30. When it's finished it will be on this page, and not before.

How we do business

Be the number they call the next ten times.

The goal on any job isn't to make as much as possible on that job. It's to be the number a project manager calls the next ten times, and the ten after that.

01

Price it once and hold it

Flat fee, no rent that keeps running, no removal charge, no excessive damage fees. Every one of those is a profit center at a national company, and we decided not to build one.

02

Move when the site moves

Fence relocated, gates shifted, a run added to an existing job. Same-day service for general contractors, because a fence in the wrong place stops other trades.

03

Answer the phone

Richard takes the calls himself, for sales and for service. A live person on the first ring is most of what separates us from a dispatch queue.

04

Say no plainly

We don't do permanent fence installation, gate automation, residential fencing, fence repairs, stanchions, orange fencing or fence material sales. If you need one of those, we'll say so on the call and point you somewhere useful.
