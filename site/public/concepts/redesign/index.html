How it works

From first call to pickup, in five steps.

Ninety-nine percent of our work is business to business, so the process is built around a project manager who needs a number for a bid and a date to hold a sub to.

  1. 01

    Tell us five things

    Location, fence style, linear feet, number of gates, and how long you need it.

  2. 02

    Get a number the same day you call

    Verbally first, because you need it for a bid. The written proposal follows.

  3. 03

    We schedule the install

    24 to 48 hours is normal. Emergency work moves faster.

  4. 04

    We stay reachable

    Need a gate moved or a run added? Call and we come out, same day for general contractors.

  5. 05

    We pull it on your schedule

    There’s no extra fee to collect it. Removal was in the number.
