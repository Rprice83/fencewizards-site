Privacy Policy

When you tell us about a project, you share information we need to help plan and price your fencing. Here’s how this website handles it.

Draft for the private website preview · September 30, 2026

This policy describes the preview and identifies features planned for launch. Richard still needs to confirm the final service providers and record-retention practices. Preview contact forms use a testing account; inquiries may be received by the website development team. Please do not submit sensitive information.

Information you provide

Contact forms ask for details such as your name, company, email address, phone number, project location, fencing needs, timing, and message. You choose what to include in any optional fields. Please do not send payment-card details, passwords, or other sensitive information through these forms.

The fence planner uses the measurements, fence lines, gate positions, rental options, and other project details you enter to calculate an estimate. Planning and pricing run in your browser. In this static preview, submitting a planner request is disabled, and demonstration receipts and inbox entries use fictional information.

How information is used

Information submitted through the contact form is used to respond to your inquiry and discuss the work you need. Once the quote system is connected, submitted project information will also support reviewing estimates, arranging service, keeping project records, and protecting the system against misuse.

Forms, hosting, and service providers

The current contact form sends submissions through Web3Forms to the configured testing recipient. This private preview is hosted through OpenAI Sites. The hosting and form services may process technical information, such as an IP address and browser information, to deliver and protect their services. Access to the private preview may require signing in with its hosting provider.

The planned production setup uses Cloudflare for hosting, quote storage, staff access, and spam protection, with Resend for quote-related email. These connections are not active in this static preview. This policy will be updated to reflect the services actually used before launch.

Google Maps and address suggestions are not connected yet. No payment service is connected to the planner. Links to Google reviews and other external websites take you to services with their own privacy practices.

Saved quotes and private receipt links

Once connected, the quote system will save submitted contact details, the project address, fence layout, gates, rental selections, notes, estimate calculations, and quote status. Authorized staff will use that information to review your request.

The planned receipt link will let anyone who has it view the project location, layout, and estimate. It will not display your contact email, phone number, or private staff notes. Treat the link as private. Receipt links are designed to expire after 30 days and can be revoked earlier. Expiration prevents access through that link; it does not delete the saved quote.

Browser storage and shared plans

The quote experience uses temporary browser session storage to help maintain a submission and reopen its receipt during that browser session. Hosting and security services may also use cookies or similar technologies needed to operate their services.

If you export or share a plan, its drawing and rental selections are included in the file or share link. The planner’s shared plan does not include the contact details, address, or notes entered into the request form. Only share a plan with people you intend to receive it.

Keeping and removing information

A record-retention period has not yet been finalized for launch. The prepared quote system does not automatically delete records when a receipt link expires. If you have submitted information during testing and would like it reviewed or removed, contact us using the details below.

You can also ask about information you have provided, request a correction, or raise a privacy concern. Any request will need to be checked against the records involved and any applicable recordkeeping requirements.
